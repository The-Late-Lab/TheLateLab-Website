TheLateLab.Dev privacy policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

This notice describes personal information used by TheLateLab.Dev (package com.thelatelab.dev), an internal test application. The Late Lab development team ("we") uses it to test the reusable game packages we build before they are used in our games. It is not a game, it is not offered to the public, and it is distributed only to testers we invite through Google Play's internal or closed testing tracks. For questions or privacy requests, email contact@thelatelab.com. Service providers also publish their own privacy notices, linked below.

1. What the app is for

The app contains test screens ("showcases") for features such as achievements, leaderboards, online sessions, purchases, advertising, analytics and connecting a phone to another device. Builds change often and a feature may be enabled, disabled or replaced between versions. Testers use it to check that these features work on real Android devices.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores test progress, achievement progress, settings and similar test data on your device. This data stays on the device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

3. Google Play Games Services

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the app uses your Play Games player identifier and display name to load, unlock and show achievements and related features. Achievement state is stored by Google under your Play Games profile. You can play without signing in; progress is then kept only on the device until you sign in.

4. Online services

Some test screens use Unity Gaming Services (UGS), such as authentication, cloud save, leaderboards, friends, lobbies and relays, remote configuration and server-side code. Signing in is anonymous by default; testers can optionally create an account with a username and password or a Unity Player Account. These features use account or player identifiers, usernames, authentication information, session information and technical connection data. Test features that link your phone to another device exchange session codes and connection data through these services or directly over your network. Network service providers receive information such as your IP address when your device connects. Leaderboard and multiplayer test screens may show a player name and scores to other testers.

5. Analytics and diagnostics

Test builds may send usage and performance information to UGS Analytics, such as device and session information, build identifiers, the test screens used and test events, so we can check that the packages work. Crash and diagnostic information may be collected by Google Play and Unity to help us find faults. We do not use this information to identify testers personally beyond what is needed to investigate a problem.

6. Advertising and purchases

Test builds may show test advertisements through Unity LevelPlay and its ad networks. Ad providers can use the device advertising identifier and technical information to deliver ads; you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Purchase screens use Google Play Billing, normally with Google's test payment methods. Payment details are handled by Google; we only receive the purchase result needed to grant the test item.

7. Support

If you email us, we receive your address, message and any files you choose to send. Please do not send passwords or authentication tokens. Our business mailbox is hosted by Hostinger.

8. Why information is used and who receives it

We use this information only to test and improve our packages and to answer testers. Recipients are Google (Google Play, Play Games Services, Play Billing), Unity (UGS and LevelPlay) and the ad networks LevelPlay works with, Hostinger for correspondence, and team members who need the information for these purposes. Providers may process information in countries other than yours. We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information when required by law.

9. Retention and your choices

Local data stays on your device until you remove it. Data kept by Google or Unity follows those services' retention rules; test data we control is deleted when it is no longer needed for testing. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, and complain to your local data-protection authority. Send requests to contact@thelatelab.com, identifying the app and, where possible, your player identifier. To delete your account and its data, follow the steps on the account deletion page. You can stop testing at any time by leaving the test track and uninstalling the app.
