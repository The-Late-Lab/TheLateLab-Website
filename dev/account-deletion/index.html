Delete your TheLateLab.Dev account and data

Last updated November 10, 2026

This page explains how to delete the account and associated data of TheLateLab.Dev, the internal Android test app published by The Late Lab on Google Play. You do not need to install or open the app to make a request.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to contact@thelatelab.com with the subject TheLateLab.Dev account deletion.
  2. Include the username you used in the app and, if you can, your player ID (shown on the app's account screen). If you only signed in with Google Play Games, include the Google Play Games name shown in the app.
  3. We may reply to confirm that the account is yours before deleting it. Do not send passwords or identity documents.

We complete deletion within 30 days of confirming the request and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account with our online services (Unity Gaming Services authentication), including your username and linked sign-in methods.
  • Data stored online for that account: cloud saves, leaderboard entries, friends and lobby information.
  • Analytics data associated with your player ID, through Unity's deletion process.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Your deletion request email, kept for up to 12 months as a record that the request was handled, then deleted.
  • Aggregated or anonymized statistics that no longer identify you.
  • Information we must keep to meet legal obligations, only for as long as the law requires.

Data we do not control

  • Data on your device (local progress and settings): uninstall the app, or open Android Settings → Apps → TheLateLab.Dev → Storage → Clear data.
  • Google Play Games (achievements and your Play Games profile) is managed by Google. You can delete your Play Games data from the Play Games app or your Google Account settings.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

See the TheLateLab.Dev privacy policy for more about how the app uses data.
