Move by the rules. Escape unseen.

A compact turn-based stealth puzzle game where familiar chess movement becomes the language of infiltration, prediction, and escape.

Chess movement. Stealth decisions.

Plan the route before the board closes in.

Guide your pieces to green exits while enemy pieces patrol and capture using familiar chess movement. You move one piece, the enemy responds, and every action changes which squares are safe.

Stealthmate is not traditional chess: there are no armies or checkmates. Chess rules provide a readable movement system for short, deterministic infiltration puzzles.

Perfect information Read current and projected danger before you commit.
Short tactical puzzles Restart quickly, learn the board, and find a cleaner route.
Campaign + Daily Challenges Authored puzzles alongside repeatable daily play.

Tip: select a friendly piece, inspect the highlighted legal moves, and get the required pieces to the green exits.
