Gameplay

See Stealthmate in action

Plan routes, read threats, and turn familiar chess movement into a stealth toolkit.

Chess movement. Stealth decisions.

Plan the route before the board closes in.

Guide your pieces to green exits while enemy pieces patrol and capture using familiar chess movement. You move one piece, the enemy responds, and every action changes which squares are safe.

Chess movement powers both compact infiltration puzzles and real-time Versus. Play the campaign solo or in local co-op, then compete against bots or other players locally or online on different maps with configurable rules.
