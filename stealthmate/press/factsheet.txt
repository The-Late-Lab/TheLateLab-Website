Chess movement. Stealth decisions.
Stealthmate turns familiar chess movement into bite-sized stealth heists: read the patrols, find your route, and escape with your pieces.
Platform: Windows PC (Steam). Languages: English, French, Italian, German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese.
Demo modes: single-player puzzles/async campaign, local co-op, bot/local/online Versus. Native online co-op is not included. Remote Play Together streams local modes.
