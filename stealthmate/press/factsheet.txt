Stealthmate: A chess puzzle — The Late Lab
Studio: The Late Lab is an independent studio of three siblings from Colombia.
Press contact: contact@thelatelab.com
Chess movement. Stealth decisions.
Solve stealth puzzles using chess movement, evade patrols, and tackle the campaign solo or in local co-op. You can also compete in real-time chess, with different maps and configurable rules, against bots, locally or online.
Platform: Windows PC (Steam). Languages: English, French, Italian, German, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese.
Demo modes: single-player puzzles/async campaign, local co-op, bot/local/online Versus. Native online co-op is not included. Remote Play Together streams local modes.
