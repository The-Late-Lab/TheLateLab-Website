About the game

Guide chess pieces through handcrafted boards where movement is a tool for infiltration rather than traditional checkmate. Read enemy patrols, avoid capture, collect fragments, and look for a cleaner escape. Turn-based puzzles and live-movement challenges test your planning and timing.

Steam Demo modes

  • Single-player puzzles and asynchronous campaign challenges.
  • Local two-player co-op campaign.
  • Bot/local/online Versus, including invitations and quick match.
  • Steam Cloud progress, in-game leaderboards and Remote Play Together for local modes.

Native online co-op is not included. Remote Play Together streams local play.
